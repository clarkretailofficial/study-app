Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Who we are

ScribeStack ("we", "us", "our") provides a note-taking and AI-assisted study tool for students. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

2. Information we collect

  • Account info: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Content you create: your notes, folder names and colors, and (on Premium) any files you upload.
  • Preferences & usage: your theme and text-size settings, and basic account activity needed to run the app.
  • Payment information (Premium only): handled entirely by our payment processor; we do not store your full card number.

We don't collect more than we need to run the app, and we don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: storing and displaying your notes and folders, letting you log in, and so on.
  • To generate AI study material (Premium): when you ask us to turn a note into flashcards, a quiz, or a test, the text of that note is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic's Claude API) solely to generate that material. It is not used to train any AI model and is not retained by us beyond what's needed to deliver the result back to you.
  • To communicate with you: password resets, service updates, and (if you opt in) product announcements.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

4. Data retention & deletion

We aim to keep only the data we actually need, for as long as you need it:

  • While your account is active, your notes, folders, and files (Premium) are kept so you can access them anytime.
  • If you delete your account, everything tied to it — notes, folders, files, and your account record — is permanently deleted from our systems.
  • If you cancel a Premium subscription, your Premium access continues through the end of the billing period you already paid for. Once that period ends, your account converts to the Free plan. Because the Free plan is limited to 10 notes, any notes beyond your 10 most recently updated, and any uploaded files, that you haven't downloaded by the time your Premium period ends will be permanently deleted from our systems. We'll do our best to remind you before this happens, but we'd still recommend downloading anything you want to keep ahead of time.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run ScribeStack, and only as needed:

  • Anthropic (Claude API), to generate the AI study material you request.
  • Our payment processor, to handle Premium subscription billing.
  • Our hosting provider, to run the servers that store your data.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

6. Your rights

You can access, update, download, or delete your notes and account at any time from within the app (see Settings). If you'd like help with any of this, or have questions about your data, contact us at [contact email].

7. Children's privacy

ScribeStack is intended for students 13 and older. If you are under 18, you should have a parent or guardian's permission to use ScribeStack. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your information, including hashed passwords and secure sessions. No system is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as ScribeStack changes. We'll update the date at the top when we do, and let you know about significant changes.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at [contact email].
